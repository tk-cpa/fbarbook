FBAR for Hipsters

Hipster moved abroad, opened a few accounts, started a company, and fell in love. Now the US government wants to know about all of it. This is the story of how he found out -- and what the law actually says.

FBAR for Hipsters is a general-education story, not tax or legal advice for any specific person's facts. FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) rules depend on account ownership, signature authority, aggregate value, and entity structure, all of which vary by situation.
