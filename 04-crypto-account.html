Chapter 4

The Crypto Exchange Account

Where the law is genuinely unsettled, and why "probably fine" isn't a filing position

Hipster also has an account at a crypto exchange based outside the US, holding nothing but digital assets. No dollars, no local currency, just crypto. It's the account he worried about most, and the one with the least settled answer.

FinCEN is unusually direct here. In Notice 2020-2 it said: "a foreign account holding virtual currency is not reportable on the FBAR (unless it is a reportable account under 31 C.F.R. 1010.350 because it holds reportable assets besides virtual currency)." It also said it intends to propose amending the regulations to add virtual currency as a reportable account type. As of October 2026, there is no final rule, and FinCEN's regulatory agenda entry for FBAR amendments (RIN 1506-AB26) doesn't mention virtual currency.

So today, crypto-only means not reportable. Two catches. First, that answer rests on a 2020 notice about a rule FinCEN plans to change, with no promised timing or transition. Second, the exception covers only accounts holding nothing but virtual currency. Add a cash balance or any other reportable asset and the whole account is reportable, like any other foreign account.

A general-audience book shouldn't settle this. It turns on exactly what the account holds and the state of the rulemaking on the due date.

The Law, Actually

  • FinCEN Notice 2020-2 -- a foreign account holding only virtual currency is not currently reportable on the FBAR, unless it also holds reportable assets besides virtual currency.
  • FinCEN Notice 2020-2 -- FinCEN stated its intent to propose amending 31 CFR 1010.350 to add virtual currency as a type of reportable account; no final rule as of October 2026; the Unified Agenda entry for FBAR amendments (RIN 1506-AB26) does not mention virtual currency.

Flagged for Verification

This is a live regulatory area, not a settled one. Before relying on "crypto-only foreign accounts are not reportable" for a real filing, confirm the status of FinCEN's virtual currency rulemaking for the year being filed, and confirm exactly what the account holds (crypto-only or mixed).

Bottom Line

Right now, an account holding only foreign crypto sits outside the FBAR's definition of a reportable account, per FinCEN's 2020 notice. A mixed account doesn't get that treatment. This is the one chapter where "check again before you file" is the actual advice, not a hedge.
